A website doesn't last forever. Yours might be working against you right now, quietly driving customers away. Here are seven signs that don't lie.

## 1. It's slow to load

If your site takes more than 3 seconds to appear, you lose visitors before they ever see your offer. Speed means sales and search ranking.

## 2. It's not mobile-friendly

Most of your visitors arrive on their phone. If your site forces them to zoom, scroll sideways or pinch the screen, they leave.

## 3. It's invisible on Google

Search for your business and can't find it? Your competitors can be found. A poorly structured site with no SEO stays buried in the results.

## 4. It looks dated

A design that screams 2014 sends the wrong signal: if the site is neglected, what about the service? First impressions happen in seconds.

## 5. It generates no inquiries

Traffic but no calls or emails? That's often a structure and call-to-action problem. A good site guides the visitor toward one clear action.

## 6. You can't edit it

If changing a single line means calling someone (or isn't possible at all), your site has become a prison rather than a tool.

## 7. It's not bilingual (and you need it to be)

In Quebec and Canada, speaking to a single language market means leaving half your potential customers with the competition.

## What a redesign changes

A proper redesign isn't just "freshening up the look." It fixes the foundations: speed, structure, SEO, conversion. The result is a site that loads fast, ranks, and turns visitors into customers.
