Having a website is one thing. Having a website that turns visitors into customers is another. If yours brings in people but no calls or emails, here are the usual culprits.

## 1. The message isn't clear in 5 seconds

When a visitor lands, they ask one question: "Is this for me?" If your home page doesn't immediately say what you do and who it's for, they leave. A clear headline beats a thousand visual effects.

## 2. There's no obvious call to action

Many sites forget to tell the visitor what to do next. Every page should push toward one specific action: request a quote, call, email. A single clear button, repeated in the right places, changes everything.

## 3. The site is too slow

A visitor won't wait. If the page takes more than a few seconds to load, they're already gone. Speed isn't a technical luxury, it's a baseline requirement for converting.

## 4. It doesn't build trust

No proof, no faces, no work to show? The visitor hesitates. Testimonials, project examples, your credentials and a real About page reassure them and make them want to reach out.

## 5. The mobile experience is poor

Most of your visitors are on their phone. If the buttons are tiny, the text unreadable or the form painful to fill out, you lose half your potential customers before they even try.
